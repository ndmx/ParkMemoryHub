# Park Memory Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

## Overview

Park Memory Hub helps people share trip memories, plans, and optional location updates with the members of their circle.

We designed the app to use on-device storage together with Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services for sync and sharing.

## Information We Handle

Park Memory Hub may handle the following information when you choose to use related features:

- Profile information, such as your display name and profile photo
- Circle information, such as your circle name and circle code
- Memories you create, including captions, tags, attached photos, and optional memory locations
- Planner items you create, including titles, notes, times, places, votes, and sharing choices
- Live or recent location information, only when you enable location sharing or choose to use your current location
- Basic app identifiers needed to distinguish devices and circle members

## How Information Is Used

We use this information to:

- Show your profile and circle membership inside the app
- Save memories on your device
- Sync shared memories, plans, and circle updates through iCloud
- Let circle members view shared plans and shared location updates
- Help you attach your current location to a memory or plan when you request it

## Photos and Memories

When you select photos for a memory, the app reads the selected photo data from your photo library with your permission.

Photos attached to memories are stored locally on your device. If you share a memory with your circle, the memory content and attached media may also be uploaded through Apple's CloudKit service so the people in your circle can access that shared memory on their devices.

## Location Data

Location access is optional.

Park Memory Hub uses location only when you:

- ask the app to use your current location for a memory or plan, or
- enable location sharing for radar

If you do not enable these features, the app does not need your live location for normal use.

## iCloud and CloudKit

Shared circle data is synchronized using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services.

Because Park Memory Hub currently uses CloudKit to share circle updates between people, shared content may be stored in Apple's cloud infrastructure to support syncing between devices and circle members.

Apple may process data as needed to provide its cloud services. Please also review Apple's privacy materials for iCloud and CloudKit.

## Sharing Controls

You control what you share:

- Memories can remain only on your device until you choose to share them in the app flow
- Planner items can be shared with your whole circle, selected members, or only yourself
- Location sharing can be turned on or off in the app
- You can leave a circle and move this device to a new circle

## Data Retention

Local app data stays on your device until you remove it, delete the app, or overwrite it through normal app use.

Shared circle data synced through CloudKit may remain available to circle members who already have access to that circle's shared content until it is deleted from the shared circle records.

## Children

Park Memory Hub is not intended to collect personal information from children without the involvement of a parent, guardian, or family organizer.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. The latest version should be published at the URL listed for the app.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support contact or support page published for Park Memory Hub.
